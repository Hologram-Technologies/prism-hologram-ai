# KN-87, the rotary frequencies

Row 13's S7, second part: each pair's rotary frequency, in units of 2^-64 of
a turn per position, as `rope_frequencies` in `crates/exact` gives it.

`Engine.Stage.RopeFrequency::plain theta headDim i` is
`theta^(-2i / headDim) / (2 pi)`, generated in integers over 2^62 the way the
Rust does: `log2Fixed` by halving to the whole part and one bit per squaring
for 62 bits; `pow2Fixed` as the product of `2^(2^-j)`, each the integer root
of the one before (the digit loop of KN-85), shifted by the whole part; `pi`
from KN-86's Machin series cut to 2^62. `llama3` scales it as Llama 3
does: kept above `high / original` turns per position, divided by `factor`
below `low / original`, and blended linearly between, each division rounded
half to even.

The Rust refuses an odd or zero head size, a `theta` below 2, a scaling past
its bounds or a frequency of 2^64 or more; the stage is stated for the
inputs within them.

## What Lean evaluates

Pair 0 is `2^64 / (2 pi)`; Llama 3's last pair is the plain one over 32.
