# KN-85, RMS norm

`Engine.Stage.RmsNorm::run` is row 13's S9, the work of `rms_norm` in
`crates/exact`: the step KN-43 (the integer root) and KN-44 (the rounded
quotient) check, now computed. Values and weights arrive as signed 64-bit
integers in units of 2^-16, two little-endian words each, with `count` and
`epsilon` (units of 2^-32).

The sum of squares plus `count epsilon` is exact. `r` is the integer root of
that sum times 2^32 over `count`, taken by `integerRoot`, the digit-by-digit
loop `integer_root` runs: 64 steps from the bit 2^126, a pair of bits a
step. Each output is the value times its weight times 2^16 over `r`, rounded
half to even by KN-81's `divideEven`, with the sign of the product. A row
whose root is 0 gives no words, where the Rust refuses it. The Rust's other
refusals (an empty row, values or weights of 2^31 or more, more than 2^16
values, epsilon of 2^32 or more) bound its inputs; the stage is stated for
the rows within them.
