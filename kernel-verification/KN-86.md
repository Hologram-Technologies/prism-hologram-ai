# KN-86, the rotary sine and angle

Row 13's S7, first part: the integers rotary positions are built from, as
`crates/exact` builds them (EN-45).

- `sineEntry r` is the quarter-wave table's entry `r`, the integer nearest
  `2^30 sin(pi r / 32768)`: `pi` by Machin's formula (`16 atan(1/5) -
  4 atan(1/239)`), each arctangent by its alternating series, and the sine
  by its Taylor series, all in integers over 2^120 and cut down where the
  Rust cuts down, then rounded half to even to 2^-30. The table is this
  function; no 16,385-entry literal is carried.
- `sineTurn angle` and `cosineTurn angle` take an angle in units of 2^-32 of
  a turn, round it half to even to a step of 2^-16 of a turn, and read the
  table by quadrant (mirrored in the second and fourth, negated in the third
  and fourth), as `sine_turn` and `cosine_turn` do; a negative value is its
  32-bit two's complement.
- `ropeAngle position frequency` is `position frequency` modulo 2^64,
  rounded half to even from 2^-64 to 2^-32 of a turn, as `rope_angle`.

Not yet: the frequencies with Llama 3 scaling (`rope_frequencies`) and the
rotation of a head's pairs, which rounds through KN-62's `Binary32::round`.
