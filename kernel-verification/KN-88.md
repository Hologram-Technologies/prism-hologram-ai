# KN-88, rotating a head's pairs

Row 13's S7, third part: `rotate` in `crates/cpu-decode`, which turns each
query and key head by its position before attention. Pair `i` is the head's
value `i` and value `i + d/2`; its angle is KN-86's `ropeAngle` of the
position at the pair's frequency (KN-87), its cosine and sine KN-86's
`cosineTurn` and `sineTurn` in units of 2^-30.

`Engine.Stage.Rotate::firstsFrom` gives each pair's first output,
`x cos - y sin`, and `secondsFrom` its second, `y cos + x sin`: each product of
a binary32 value and a turn is exact at 2^-179 (a binary32's least unit times
2^-30), the two are summed with their signs, and the sum is rounded once by
KN-62's `Binary32::round`, as the Rust's two `Row`s and `round_to_binary32`.

## What Lean proves, and what is tested

| Theorem | For |
| --- | --- |
| `rotate_first_is_word`, `rotate_second_is_word` | every input: each output is at most `0xff800000` |
| `rotate_firsts_count`, `rotate_seconds_count` | every head: one output a pair in each half |
| `rotate_firsts_row_0` to `_3`, `rotate_seconds_row_0` to `_3` | four heads evaluated by Lean: position 0 (each pair unchanged), 1, 300, and a six-value head at 4095 |

That the stage equals the Rust is tested on those rows, not proved for every
input.

## The pinned test: `kernel_kn_88`

`rotate` is private to cpu-decode, and making it public changes the engine's
bytes and so its seal. `kernel_kn_88` therefore pins `rotate`'s arithmetic
lines in cpu-decode's source and repeats them on `crates/exact`'s
`rope_angle`, `cosine_turn`, `sine_turn`, `Row` and `round_to_binary32`, then
requires the words Lean evaluated.

| Planted defect | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `cosine_turn` turning an eighth rather than a quarter (`1 << 29`) | rejected: `cpu-decode's rotation disagrees with the stage` at `rotate_firsts_row_0` |
| none | passed |
