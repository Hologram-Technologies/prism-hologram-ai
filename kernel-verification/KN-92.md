# KN-92, the logits head

The last product of a step: the final norm's output times the head's weights,
one logit a token id. cpu-decode's `Slice::product` calls `crates/exact`'s
`dense_product_with` with `OutputMajor` rows and the 16-bit grouped scheme.
The input goes onto KN-61's codes, one power of two a group of 32; each row of
weights, f16 or binary32, is multiplied by them exactly, each group at its own
power, and each logit is rounded once.

`Engine.Stage.Head::head` is that. Each weight is an integer at 2^-149: an f16
as its `half_fixed` value (units of 2^-24) times 2^125, a binary32 as its
mantissa times 2^(scale - 1). Each code times its weight is placed at 2^-349
by `2^(200 - p)` for its group's power `p` (KN-61's power is at most 164, and
a negative one adds), so every logit is one exact signed sum, rounded once by
KN-62's `Binary32::round`. The input's length is a multiple of 32; a bfloat16
head is not covered (the Rust's head is halves or binary32).

## What Lean proves, and what is tested

| Theorem | For |
| --- | --- |
| `head_logit_is_word` | every input: each logit is at most `0xff800000` |
| `head_count` | every head: one logit a row |
| `head_row_0` to `_2` | three heads evaluated by Lean, codes and powers included: one group of f16 weights, two groups of very different size with f16 subnormal weights, one group of binary32 weights |

That the stage equals the Rust is tested on those heads, not proved for every
input.

## The pinned test: `kernel_kn_92`

`kernel_kn_92` pins cpu-decode's head call and scheme and replays each head
through `crates/exact`'s public `dense_product_with`. Long lists are written as
joined lists of 16 to stay within the module's JSON nesting.

| --- | --- |
