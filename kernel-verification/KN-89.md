# KN-89, the residual and bias additions

`add` in `crates/cpu-decode` sums two binary32 values exactly and rounds once:
the residual stream after attention and after the feed-forward, and each
q, k, v bias (read from its half first) added to its projection.
`Engine.Stage.Residual::addWord` is the same: each value as a signed integer
at 2^-149 (the least binary32 unit), the two summed with their signs, and
the sum rounded once by KN-62's `Binary32::round`; an exact zero is +0, as
the Rust's.

## What Lean proves, and what is tested

| Theorem | For |
| --- | --- |
| `residual_add_is_word` | every pair of values: the sum is at most `0xff800000` |
| `residual_add_commutes` | every pair of values: `a + b` and `b + a` are the same word |
| `residual_count` | every pair of rows: one sum a pair |
| `residual_row_0` | nine sums evaluated by Lean: 1 + 2, 1 + (-1) = +0, an absorbed 1, two least subnormals, a tie to even at 2^24 + 1, -0 + -0 = +0, overflow to infinity, -2.5 + 0.75, an absorbed tiny addend |

That the stage equals the Rust is tested on that row, not proved for every
input.

## The pinned test: `kernel_kn_89`

`add` is private to cpu-decode (making it public moves the engine's seal), so
`kernel_kn_89` pins its lines in cpu-decode's source, repeats them on
`crates/exact`'s `Row` and `round_to_binary32`, and requires the words Lean
evaluated; it also pins `residual_add_commutes`.

| Planted defect | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `round_to_binary32` rounding a tie up (`if guard {`) | rejected: `residual_row_0: cpu-decode's add disagrees with the stage` |
| none | passed |
