# KN-62, one attention head

`Engine.Stage.Attention::attend` is row 13's S10, the attention loop of
`step` in `crates/cpu-decode`, now `attend_head`, which `advance` calls once
per query head. A head reads its query, the cached keys and values of its
key-value head `hq / group`, at `(t kv_heads + kv) head_dim` for each of the
`seen` positions, and gives `head_dim` binary32 words.

Each score is the query's binary32 products with a key, summed exactly as
signed integers at 2^-298, the least unit two binary32 values multiply to,
and rounded once by `Engine.Stage.Binary32::round`. Its softmax exponent is
`score log2(e) floor(2^40 / sqrt(d)) 2^8`, rounded half to even and clamped
to `i64`, written as the two words KN-81's softmax reads. Each output is the
weights (units of 2^-30) times the cached values, summed exactly at 2^-179
and rounded once.

`Engine.Stage.Binary32::round negative n k fuel` is the binary32 nearest
(-1)^negative n 2^-k, ties to even, as `exact::round_to_binary32`: the field
is `max(bits(n) + 125 - k, 0)`, the significand is `n` rounded half to even
at the unit that field fixes, and their sum, capped at infinity, takes the
sign; zero is +0, and a negative value that rounds to nothing is -0. `fuel`
bounds the bit count; the stage passes 1280, past any sum a head forms.

## What Lean proves

| Theorem | For |
| --- | --- |
| `attention_score_is_word`, `attention_out_is_word` | every input: each score and output is at most `0xff800000` |
| `attention_head_count` | every head: exactly `head_dim` words |
| `attention_encoded_below`, `attention_high_word`, `attention_low_word` | every score: its exponent is below 2^64 and both its words are u32 words |
| `binary32_round_is_word` | every value the rounding takes |
| `attention_head_row_0` to `_3` | four heads evaluated by Lean: equal weights, one position, unequal scores, a second key-value head |
| `binary32_row_0` to `_11` | twelve roundings evaluated by Lean: ties to even, the least subnormal, overflow to infinity, -0, zero |

The values the rows equal are the Rust's: `kernel_kn_62` replays each head
through `attend_head` and each rounding through `exact::Row` and
`round_to_binary32` and requires the same words.
