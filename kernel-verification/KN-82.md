# KN-82, a transformers.js encoder call

A 4-byte request carries an encoder call as transformers.js names it: the task
(1 feature-extraction, 2 text-classification), the pooling (0 none, 1 mean, 2
cls, 255 any other) and whether to normalize. The module's encoder gives one
thing per task: a mean-pooled, unit-length embedding, or label scores. So the
kernel answers `run` exactly for feature-extraction with mean pooling and
normalizing, and for text-classification with neither, and otherwise refuses
by name: `refused: pooling`, `refused: normalize` or `refused: unsupported
task`. `site/app/engine/transformers.mjs` asks it before each encoder call and
decides nothing itself.

| Claim | Evidence | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each task runs with exactly the options its answer has; any other pooling, an unnormalized embedding, options on a classification and any other task are refused | `encode_embedding_runs`, `encode_embedding_pooling_refused`, `encode_embedding_normalize_refused`, `encode_classification_runs`, `encode_classification_options_refused`, `encode_task_refused` | proved (lexlean 0.3.0 verify in WSL, lock current, 2026-11-20; the acceptance run is the canonical check) |
