# KN-93, a q4 product

Every q4 projection of a layer (q, k, v, output, gate, up, down) is
`crates/exact`'s `q4_product_with`, which cpu-decode's `Slice::product` calls
with the 16-bit grouped scheme. It is S1, S2, S3 and S3's finish in one: the
input on KN-61's codes and powers; each unit's sum of codes times four-bit
weights less the block's zero point (S1); times the block's scale (S2);
placed at its power and summed exactly (S3); rounded once (KN-63).

`Engine.Stage.Q4Product::product` is that, as one exact sum a row: each code
times its weight (the nibble, low half of a byte first, less the zero point:
8 when none is stored, else the packed nibble for its row and block) times its
block's scale (f16 or binary32, at 2^-149, as KN-92's head reads weights),
placed at 2^-349 by its group's power, and the row rounded once by KN-62's
`Binary32::round`. The input's length is a multiple of 32 and a block at least
32 wide, as the exports have it.

## What Lean proves, and what is tested

| Theorem | For |
| --- | --- |
| `q4_row_is_word` | every input: each output is at most `0xff800000` |
| `q4_count` | every product: one output a row |
| `q4_row_0` to `_2` | three products evaluated by Lean, codes and powers included: one block of 32 with f16 scales and the default zero point; two blocks with binary32 scales and packed zero points; one block of 64 read in two units of 32 with packed zero points |

That the stage equals the Rust is tested on those products, not proved for
every input.

## The pinned test: `kernel_kn_93`

`kernel_kn_93` pins cpu-decode's call, builds `crates/exact`'s `Q4Matrix` from
the words Lean read, and replays each product through the public
`q4_product_with`.

| --- | --- |
