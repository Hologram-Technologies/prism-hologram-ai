# KN-90, the feed-forward's fixed-point entry and gated product

The gated feed-forward in `crates/cpu-decode` takes each gate value `g` onto
units of 2^-16 (`shift_even(g.mantissa, g.exponent + 16)`, rounded half to
even), applies SiLU (KN-83), multiplies by the up value `u` exactly and rounds
once (`round_exact(a u.mantissa, u.exponent - 16)`).

`Engine.Stage.Gated::entry` is the first step: a binary32's mantissa times
2^(scale - 134), or divided by 2^(134 - scale) rounded half to even by KN-81's
`divideEven`, with its sign as a 64-bit two's complement word, the form
`Engine.Stage.Silu::silu` reads. `gatedWord` is the rest: the SiLU's magnitude
times the up value's mantissa at 2^-165, the two signs combined, rounded once
by KN-62's `Binary32::round`. A gate value too large for 64 bits at 2^-16 is
refused by the Rust (`NormRefused`); the stage is stated for the values
within it.

## What Lean proves, and what is tested

| Theorem | For |
| --- | --- |
| `gated_is_word` | every gate and up value: the product is at most `0xff800000` |
| `gated_entry_count`, `gated_count` | every row: one output an input |
| `gated_entry_row_0`, `gated_row_0` | eight pairs evaluated by Lean: 1.0, -2.5, 0, a value below 2^-17 (entry 0), 30,000, a small negative, 7.0, -0 |

That the stage equals the Rust is tested on those rows, not proved for every
input.

## The pinned test: `kernel_kn_90`

`shift_even` and the gated step are private to cpu-decode (making either public
moves the engine's seal), so `kernel_kn_90` pins their lines in cpu-decode's
source, repeats them on `crates/exact`'s `silu` and `round_exact`, and
requires the words Lean evaluated.

| Planted defect | Result |
| --- | --- |
| `round_exact` placing the value at half its power (`power - 1`) | rejected: `gated_row_0: cpu-decode gives ...` |
| none | passed |
