# KN-63, a row rounded to binary32

`Engine.Stage.RowFinish::run` is the end of row 13's S3: KN-59's register,
twelve little-endian words a row, becomes the row's binary32 output. The
twelve words are a 384-bit two's-complement value whose bit 0 weighs
2^-192; a value of 2^383 or more is negative, of magnitude 2^384 less it.
`Engine.Stage.Binary32::round` (KN-62) rounds it once, ties to even, with
subnormals, infinity past the largest finite value, +0 for zero and -0 for a
negative value too small to keep. This is the rounding `q4_product_with`
applies to each row in `crates/exact`, and the one GPU kernel `q4Finish`
(KN-73) is to equal.

## What Lean proves

| Theorem | For |
| --- | --- |
| `row_finish_is_word` | every register: the output is at most `0xff800000` |
| `row_finish_count` | every row count: one word a row |
| `row_finish_row_0` to `_7` | eight registers evaluated by Lean: 1.0, -1.5, a value below the least subnormal and its negative (-0), overflow, a tie to even, a round up, the least subnormal |

## The pinned test: `kernel_kn_63`

`kernel_kn_63` reads each row Lean evaluated, rebuilds the signed register
in `exact::Row` word by word from 2^-192 up, and requires
`round_to_binary32` to give the same word.
